Most people try AI, get something bland back, and give up.

The problem usually isn't the tool. It's that nobody showed them four simple habits:

→ Delegation: decide what to hand over, and what to keep
→ Description: say clearly what you want
→ Discernment: judge what comes back
→ Diligence: use it responsibly, and own the result

These come from Anthropic's free AI Fluency course, and they're the clearest way I've found to explain what "good with AI" actually means.

I've started a newsletter, Field Notes, about using AI at work in plain English. It's for people who aren't technical. Issue one is out today, on these four habits.
