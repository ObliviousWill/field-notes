If you can smell gas:

  • Call the National Gas Emergency number: 0800 111 999 (free, 24 hours). If you use LPG, call your LPG supplier's emergency number.
  • Open doors and windows to let fresh air in.
  • Don't use electrical switches, plugs or doorbells.
  • No naked flames: don't smoke, light matches or use lighters.
  • Turn off the gas at the meter if you can, and leave the property if you feel unsafe.
  • Feeling unwell (headache, dizziness, nausea)? Get into fresh air and seek medical help.
