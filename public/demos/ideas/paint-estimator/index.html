The small print (typical figures; check the tin)
  • Walls only: four walls, width × length × height. Ceilings, woodwork and alcoves are not included.
  • Each door takes off about 1.9 m² and each window about 1.5 m². Big patio doors or bay windows will change things.
  • Coverage is about 12 m² per litre per coat, a typical figure for standard emulsion on smooth, previously painted walls. Fresh plaster, rough or very dark walls drink more, so check your tin.
  • Tins come in 1, 2.5 and 5 litre sizes. We pick the combination that is likely to cost least using example prices (1 L £14, 2.5 L £26, 5 L £42). Real prices vary by brand and shop.
