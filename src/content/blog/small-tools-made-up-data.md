Most teams run on a handful of everyday questions. Are we on budget? Who's covering on Monday? Can I take the second week of August off?

The answers usually live in spreadsheets, inboxes and people's heads. Big systems can answer them, but they're expensive, slow to change and often answer a slightly different question to the one you actually have.

So I built three small tools with Claude, each answering one question well. You can try all three below.

## The three tools

**[A budget tracker](/field-notes/demos/budget/).** Planned spend against actual spend, area by area. Bars change colour as an area gets close to its budget. Logging something takes a few seconds.

**[A cover rota](/field-notes/demos/rota/).** The next fortnight at a glance: who's on each day, and how busy each day is likely to be, so the workload can be shared fairly.

**[A holiday planner](/field-notes/demos/holiday/).** Helps everyone plan their time off through the year, and spots clashes before they happen.

## A note on the data

These are demo versions. **Every name, number and date in them is made up.** The tools I use day to day hold real information about real people and real budgets, so they stay private. These copies do exactly the same things, with invented data, so you can see how they work.

Anything you change in a demo is saved only in your own browser. There's a button to reset it.

## Why small tools work

**They answer one question.** A tool that does one job is easy to understand and easy to trust. The budget tracker doesn't forecast, plan campaigns or manage invoices. It tells you where you are against the plan. That's all.

**They're quick to change.** If a team wants a new column or a different colour, it's a short conversation with Claude, not a project.

**They don't replace the official record.** The finance system and the HR system still hold the truth. These are planning views on top. That keeps them simple, and keeps everyone clear about where the real record lives.

**They're designed around privacy from the start.** The rota and holiday planner use first names only. Reasons for leave are only ever "holiday", "training" or "unavailable", so nothing personal ends up in there.

## How to build your own

If you have a question your team asks every week, try this:

1. Write down the question, and who asks it.
2. Write down where the answer currently lives.
3. Ask Claude: "Build me a simple page that answers this question. Keep it to one job."
4. Use it for a week. Then ask for the one change that would make it more useful.

Start small. The best tools are the ones people actually open.
