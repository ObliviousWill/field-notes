A boiler service is easy to forget and expensive to skip.

This checker asks a few questions about age, last service and warning signs, then gives a traffic-light verdict with clear reasons. It includes the important safety information: who's qualified to do the work, what to do if you smell gas, and why a carbon monoxide alarm matters.

The natural offer is a reminder when the next service is due.
