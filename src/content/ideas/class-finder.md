New members often don't know what a class involves, or whether they'll fit in. So they don't come.

This quiz takes six questions about energy, mood and preferences and recommends one class, with what to bring and why it suits them. It gently reminds people to check with their GP if they have health concerns.

The obvious next step is a free taster class, so that's the offer.
