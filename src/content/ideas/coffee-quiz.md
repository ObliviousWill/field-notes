A café is a local habit, and habits start with a reason to walk in.

This quiz gives people a bit of fun, a result they want to share, and a drink to try next time they're passing. The share button does the marketing for you.

The natural offer at the end is a coffee club: a free drink on your birthday, in exchange for an email.
