Working out how much paint to buy is fiddly, so people guess, and get it wrong.

This estimator does the maths: wall area minus doors and windows, times coats, divided by typical coverage, rounded up to real tin sizes. The room repaints in the chosen colour as you go, which is half the fun.

Someone who's just measured their walls is often wondering whether to do it themselves. That's the moment to offer a free quote.
