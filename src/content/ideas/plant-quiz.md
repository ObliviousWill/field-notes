Most people walk into a garden centre and pick the prettiest plant. Two weeks later it's dead and they blame themselves.

This quiz flips that. It asks about the home first, then recommends a plant that will thrive there. If you have pets, it only suggests pet-safe plants.

The result is something worth keeping: a plant with a personality, three care tips and a pet-safety note. That's the natural moment to offer a care guide by email.
