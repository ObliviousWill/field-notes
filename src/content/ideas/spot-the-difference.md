Not every idea has to be serious. A small game on your website or social media gets people spending time with your brand.

This one shows two illustrated shop windows with five differences, a timer and a hint button. Find them all and you unlock an example discount code, with a burst of confetti.

The offer is simple: email me the code, so I don't lose it.
