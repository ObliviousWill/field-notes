Every January, thousands of people realise their tax return is due and they haven't started.

This page counts down to the online Self Assessment deadline and walks people through a quick readiness checklist, with encouraging messages as the meter fills up. It explains the key dates and the late-filing penalty, without scaremongering.

The offer writes itself: a free short call before the rush.
