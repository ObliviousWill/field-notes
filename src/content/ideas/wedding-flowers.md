Wedding flowers are a big, unknown cost, and many couples put off asking a florist because they're nervous about the answer.

This calculator gives them a ballpark range as they go, with a seasonal tip. It's clear that the prices are examples and real quotes vary.

A couple who has played with their budget is much more ready to talk, so the offer is a free personalised quote.
