Money questions are usually simple: are we spending what we said we'd spend, and is this plan realistic? Getting the answer usually isn't. It's buried in spreadsheets.

These two tools make the answer quick to find.

**The spend tracker** is a single page. It shows the budget for each area of marketing, what's been spent so far and what's left. Every time money is spent, you log it in a few seconds: what it was, which area, how much and when. Bars turn amber at 90% and red when an area goes over. You can see the spending by area or by type, such as advertising or printing.

**The budget checker** helps with a once-a-year job. We work with a network of partner businesses, and each one sends us a budget for the year. Someone used to read every budget by hand. Now Claude reads them all and:

- re-adds the figures to check they're right
- compares each plan with what that business actually achieved last year
- checks it against the targets we agreed
- spots anything that looks unrealistic, such as costs far too low or growth far too high
- writes a short review for each one, with questions to ask

A person still reads every review and makes the call.
