This is the site you're on. I started with a blank page: no website builder like Wix or Squarespace, and no agency.

I describe what I want in plain English. Claude, an AI assistant, writes the code. I check the result, push back, and we go again. Every change is saved with a note of what changed and why, so you can trace the whole history of the site.

The goal isn't a polished portfolio. It's a record of decisions: what I tried, what I changed my mind about, and what worked.
