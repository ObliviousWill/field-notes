Padel nights fall apart at the scoring. Someone keeps the table in their notes app, someone else works out who partners who next round, and by round four nobody agrees on the numbers.

PISTA fixes that, and it's deliberately simple. Add the people playing and pick a format. It draws each round so everyone partners and plays against different people. You type in the points, it keeps the table, and it tells you who won.

It's part of this site rather than a separate app. Open [/pista/](/field-notes/pista/) and it works. Add it to your phone's home screen if you want it full screen, like a normal app.

Your scores are kept on your phone, so they're there next time you open it. Nothing is sent anywhere: there's no account and no database. If you want your scores on another phone, or you're about to clear your browser, there's a backup button.
