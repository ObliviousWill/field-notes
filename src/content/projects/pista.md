Padel nights fall apart at the scoring. Someone keeps the table in their notes app, someone else works out who partners who next round, and by round four nobody agrees on the numbers.

PISTA is the fix, and it is deliberately small. Add the people playing, pick a format, and it draws each round so partners and opponents rotate properly. You enter the points, it keeps the standings, and it tells you who won.

It is a separate part of this site rather than a separate product. Open [/pista/](/field-notes/pista/), add it to your home screen if you want it full screen, and it works. Everything you score is written to your browser's own storage on that device, so it comes back next time. Nothing leaves your phone: there is no account and no database behind it. History has a backup you can copy or download if you want your data on a second device, or before you clear your browser.
