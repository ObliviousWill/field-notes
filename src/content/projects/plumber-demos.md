When your boiler breaks, you're stuck. You don't know what's wrong, you don't know if it's serious, and the words the engineer uses mean nothing to you.

This demo is for a local plumber to give to their customers. It's a 3D oil boiler you can turn round with your finger:

- **Tap any of the 17 parts** to see what it does, what goes wrong with it, what you'd notice and how it gets fixed.
- **Pick a symptom**, such as "reset light keeps coming on" or "smell of oil", and the parts most likely to blame light up, most likely first.
- **Take the cover off** to see inside.

Safety notes appear where they matter. Oil work is only for a registered technician. Fit a carbon monoxide alarm. Don't press reset more than twice.

The point isn't to turn customers into plumbers. It's to help them describe the problem properly when they call, and to understand why the yearly service is worth paying for. Both of those are good for the plumber's business.

[Try it yourself](/field-notes/demos/boiler/). It works best on a phone.

## Service manuals

The plumber can also upload the service manual for each make of boiler they look after. Then a customer can ask a question about *their* boiler and get an answer from the actual manual, not general advice from the internet. The demo on this site shows the 3D explorer on its own.
