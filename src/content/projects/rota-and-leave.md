Planning who's in and who's away is one of those jobs that's easy to do on the day and hard to do in advance. I wanted my teams to be able to see ahead: which days will be busy, and how everyone's holiday plans fit together.

So I built two simple pages with Claude. You open them in a browser like any other website, with nothing to install.

**The cover rota** shows the next fortnight. Each day shows who's on, and the page gives an idea of how busy that day is likely to be. It knows that school holidays, half terms and bank holidays change how busy we are. If someone books holiday, the rota updates automatically, so nobody has to remember to change it.

**The holiday planner** helps everyone get the time off they're entitled to, spread through the year:

- Everyone can see how their holiday is spread through the year, so it's easy to plan breaks and not end up with days left over.
- It spots clashes early, such as two people who cover for each other both booking the same week.
- It checks new requests against the team's agreed guidelines, so decisions are consistent and fair.

The HR system is still the official record. These pages are for planning.

## Privacy

Rotas and holidays are about people, so I kept the information to a minimum:

- Only first names are used.
- A reason for leave is only ever "holiday", "training" or "unavailable", so no personal details end up in there.
- Only I can edit. Anyone I share it with can look but not change anything.
