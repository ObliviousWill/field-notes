This is the simplest thing on this page, and one of the most useful.

Keeping up with your industry's news is one of those jobs everyone means to do and nobody has time for. So every Monday at 7am Claude does it for me. It:

1. searches the web for the last seven days of news about children's activities, childcare and schools in the UK
2. checks the dates, so nothing is old news
3. picks the three stories that matter most to us
4. writes each one up: what happened, where it came from, why it matters to us, and an idea for an internal post about it
5. emails it to me and three colleagues

There are no special systems involved. It's a set of written instructions and a timer.

The instructions do most of the work. They say:

- Use plain English, and if a sentence wouldn't make sense to someone outside marketing, rewrite it.
- Link to the source for every claim.
- Be honest when a story is only loosely relevant to us.
- If it's a quiet week, say so rather than padding it out.

The last one matters most. A briefing that always finds three stories, even in a quiet week, soon becomes noise nobody reads.

If there's a job you do every week that involves reading things and summing them up, this is the easiest place to start with AI.
