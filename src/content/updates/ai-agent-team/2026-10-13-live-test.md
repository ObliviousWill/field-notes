Today was the first time all the helpers ran together, start to finish: each did its morning check, raised a suggestion if it had one, the checker marked them, and I got the short list.

For the first few weeks the helpers can look but not touch. They read the systems and make suggestions, and that's all. The website and writing helpers have switches written into their instructions, set to OFF. Only I can turn them on. Even then they can only act on something I've already approved.

What's still unsolved:

- **They use my login.** The rules say "don't approve anything", but nothing technical stops them. I want each helper to have its own account, with its own limits and a record of everything it did.
- **What happens to people's jobs.** Some tasks will shrink. I'd rather plan that openly, role by role, with HR involved early, than let it happen by accident.
- **Data traps.** Some of our records flatter the numbers. One system only returns 200 results at a time, so a count can look complete when it isn't. The helpers have been told about each trap, but that list will keep growing.
