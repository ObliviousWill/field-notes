Today all the helpers ran together for the first time, start to finish. Each did its morning check and raised a suggestion if it had one. The checker reviewed them, and the short list arrived.

For now we're in a careful trial. The helpers can read information and make suggestions, and that's all. They can't change anything, contact anyone or publish anything. Anything beyond that would need an explicit decision, and only for work that has already been approved.

Next, we'll watch how useful the suggestions are over the coming weeks, and refine the rules based on what the team finds helpful.
