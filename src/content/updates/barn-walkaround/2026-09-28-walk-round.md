Everything in the model is drawn in code from the planning drawings and the site plan, with no 3D files. I checked it against photos of the site.

The sun slider is the part I'd copy for any project. Questions like "where should the seating go?" or "will the kitchen get morning light?" are hard to answer from a drawing. Here you just slide the sun across the sky and look.
