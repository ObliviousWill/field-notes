*$100M Leads* went in first, then *$100M Offers* three days later. The two books lean on each other: a good offer is half of good lead generation. So each skill points to the other when a question crosses over.

The first real test was a winter campaign plan for a local landscaper (see [For local businesses](/field-notes/projects/)). I asked Claude to build the offer using the books. The difference was obvious: instead of generic tips, it worked through the books' own steps, in order, and showed its working.
