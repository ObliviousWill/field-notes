pubDate: 'Jul 29 2026'
---

Up to around thirty budgets arrive in a folder, all using the same spreadsheet template. The checker opens each one, takes out the numbers and does the boring checks first. Do the months add up to the year? Do the totals match?

Then it does the useful part: it compares each budget with what that business really did last year, product by product. A plan to double something that shrank last year gets flagged, along with a question for the owner.

Each review is a Word document, so it can be edited and talked through on a call. There's also a one-page summary of all the budgets, ranked by how much attention each one needs.

The rule written at the top of its instructions: **this helps people decide. It isn't the decision.** Every figure is marked as provisional until a person has checked it.
