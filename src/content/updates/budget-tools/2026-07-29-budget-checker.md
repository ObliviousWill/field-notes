Each plan follows the same template. The checker reads each one, compares it with the previous year, and notes anything worth discussing, along with a suggested question.

Each review is a Word document, so it can be edited and talked through together. There's also a one-page overview of all the plans.

The rule written at the top of its instructions: **this helps people decide. It isn't the decision.** Every figure is marked as provisional until a person has checked it.
