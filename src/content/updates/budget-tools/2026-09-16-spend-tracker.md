The marketing plan has a budget for each area of work. Seeing what had actually been spent in each area meant digging through spreadsheets.

The tracker is deliberately small. It does one job: planned against spent, by area.

I took one thing out on purpose. An early version tried to forecast where spending would end up by the end of the year. It made the page harder to read, and nobody used it, so it went.

You can't change the budgets on the page either. The plan is agreed elsewhere, and the tracker shouldn't make it easy to quietly move the goalposts.
