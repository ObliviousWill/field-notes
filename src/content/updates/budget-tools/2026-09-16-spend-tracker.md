The marketing plan has a budget for each area of work, and it helps to see spending against each one at a glance.

The tracker is deliberately small. It does one job: planned against spent, by area.

I took one thing out on purpose. An early version tried to forecast where spending would end up by the end of the year. It made the page harder to read, so it went. Simple beats clever.

The budgets themselves are agreed in the planning process, so the tracker shows them but doesn't change them.
