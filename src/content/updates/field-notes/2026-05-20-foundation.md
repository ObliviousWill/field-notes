Day one. The site is live, it has three pages, and the first post is up.

## What's here

- **A free website.** It's built with Astro, a free tool for making websites, and hosted on GitHub Pages, which publishes it for free. The only cost is a domain name, if I ever want one.
- **My own colours.** Cream, dark slate blue and orange, taken from a palette I'd saved on Pinterest. I tweaked them so the text is easy to read for everyone, including people with poor eyesight.
- **A logo**, made in Canva.
- **A proper post page**, with my name, the date, how long it takes to read, and a list of sections down the side on big screens.
- **Three topics only**: building, marketing and AI. The site won't let me invent a fourth by accident.

## What's next

- A page for each project, with a running diary of updates. You're reading one now.
- More posts, so the site looks lived-in before I tell people about it.
- Feedback from a few colleagues.
- Nice preview cards for when someone shares a link on LinkedIn or WhatsApp.
