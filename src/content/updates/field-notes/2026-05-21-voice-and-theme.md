The site no longer looks like the template it started from. It has its own colours, a logo, an About page that sounds like me, and the first real post.

## What changed

- **Colours that are easy to read.** The orange looks great, but orange text on cream is hard to read. So the orange is used for highlights and borders, and links use a deeper shade.
- **The logo**: a chunky orange "F", made in Canva.
- **The About page**, rewritten in my own words: what I write about, and what this site isn't.
- **The first post**: *What I learned building a website with AI*. Writing about the thing the post sits on felt like the right way to start.

## What I'm noticing

The posts need more care. Headings, quotes and spacing are still the template's defaults. That's the next job.
