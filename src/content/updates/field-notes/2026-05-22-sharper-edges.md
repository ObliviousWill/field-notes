A day of small things. None of them is big on its own. Together, they're the difference between a template and a finished site.

## What changed

- **Reading time on every post**, worked out from the word count.
- **British dates**: 20 May 2026, not May 20, 2026.
- **Links that highlight** like a marker pen when you hover over them.
- **Previous and next post links** at the bottom of each post.
- **Projects.** Each project now has its own page and diary of updates, with a feed you can follow separately from the blog.
- **Share cards.** Paste a post's link into LinkedIn or WhatsApp and you get a proper preview with the title, not a bare link.

## Tidying up

- I took out every long dash (—). Lots of AI writing uses them, and I'd started noticing them everywhere. Commas, colons and full stops do the same job.
- I added real links to LinkedIn and the feed where placeholders used to be.
- I added a menu button for phones.

## What I'm noticing

Everything works, but nothing about it stands out. Next: a proper redesign.
