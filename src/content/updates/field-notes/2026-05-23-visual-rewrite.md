A full redesign. I designed it first as a mock-up, outside the site, and then Claude built it. Underneath it's the same site, but it looks and feels completely different.

## What changed

- **Bolder headings** in a chunkier typeface, paired with a body font designed to be easy to read.
- **The name, FIELD NOTES, top left**, with the menu in the middle.
- **A colour for each topic**: orange for AI, green for building, lilac for marketing.
- **Unread dots.** Each post has a small orange dot until you've read it. Your browser remembers this, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **A reading progress bar** across the top of each post, with how many minutes you have left.
- **A timeline** for project updates, newest at the top.
- **A sign-up box** for email updates. It isn't connected to anything yet.

## What I left for later

- Pages for each topic. They're only worth it once there are more posts.
- Choosing an email newsletter service.
