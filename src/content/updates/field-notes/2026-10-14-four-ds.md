pubDate: 'Oct 14 2026'

The guides have grown from four to eleven, and they now follow a structure: the four Ds from Anthropic's AI Fluency course. **Delegation**, **Description**, **Discernment** and **Diligence**.

The first guide is still a plain "how to get started". From the second guide onwards, each one teaches one of the four Ds, building up as you go. New guides fill the gaps: deciding what to hand over, checking what comes back, teaching Claude a job once with skills, connecting it to your other tools, asking questions of your own files, handing over whole jobs, and using it all responsibly.

The guides page also has a "Stuck?" section that points common sticking points, like "the answers are too generic" or "I don't trust what it tells me", to the guide that helps.

Each guide now shows a small graphic of the four Ds near the top, with the one it covers highlighted. So you can always see where you are in the bigger picture.

I've also started writing for LinkedIn. Each newsletter issue points back to the guides and posts here.
