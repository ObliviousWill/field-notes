The guides have grown from four to seven, and they now follow a structure: the four Ds from Anthropic's AI Fluency course. **Delegation**, **Description**, **Discernment** and **Diligence**.

The first guide is still a plain "how to get started". From the second guide onwards, each one teaches one of the four Ds, building up as you go. Three new guides fill the gaps: deciding what to hand over, checking what comes back, and using AI responsibly.

Each guide now shows a small graphic of the four Ds near the top, with the one it covers highlighted. So you can always see where you are in the bigger picture.

I've also started writing for LinkedIn. Each newsletter issue points back to the guides and posts here.
