There's a new section in the menu: **Ideas**.

It's a collection of quizzes, calculators, checkers and games that small businesses could use to bring in customers. A houseplant quiz for a garden centre. A paint calculator for a decorator. A spot-the-difference game for a local shop.

Each one comes with three things:

1. **A working demo** you can play straight away.
2. **Why it works**, in a sentence.
3. **The prompt** to paste into Claude to build your own version.

The businesses are made up, and the demos don't collect anything. Each one shows where a real version would ask for an email, with a clear, unticked consent box.

I've started with eight. More will follow.
