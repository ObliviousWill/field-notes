Reading about a skill only gets you so far. So six of the guides now have a small exercise built in, each one practising exactly what that guide teaches:

- **Hand it over, or keep it?** Answer four questions about one of your own tasks and get a suggestion for how to split it.
- **Build a brief.** Fill in the parts of a good request and watch it come together, ready to copy into Claude. It tells you which parts are still missing.
- **Write your "about me".** A few questions, and you get a short description to paste into Claude's settings.
- **Spot the mistakes.** An answer of the kind AI gives, with three mistakes hidden in it. Find them, then see why each one is wrong.
- **Plan a whole job.** Break a job into steps, choose where you want to check, and get instructions to paste into Claude.
- **Safe to paste?** Paste in what you're about to give Claude, and it highlights personal details like emails, phone numbers and postcodes, and can swap them for placeholders.

Everything runs in your own browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.

Each guide also has a "Mark as done" button, so you can keep track of your progress on the guides page.
