Two changes today.

**The audio versions now use my voice.** Or rather, an AI version of it. I recorded a sample and made a voice clone with ElevenLabs, and every guide and post is now read in that voice. The player says so plainly. I also tidied how the text is turned into speech: headings get a proper pause, tables are read as sentences, and the short lines that point at the interactive tools are skipped, since you can't see them when you're listening.

**Three more tools you can try.** There are now demo versions of the budget tracker, the cover rota and the holiday planner. Every name and number in them is made up, so you can try them without seeing anyone's real information. I wrote about [why small tools work](/field-notes/blog/small-tools-made-up-data/).
