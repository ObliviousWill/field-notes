pubDate: 'Oct 14 2026'
---

I asked for an honest opinion on the design, and got one: tasteful, but it looked like a lot of other sites. Cream background, a serif heading, rounded cards. Nothing on it felt like *me*.

So the site is starting to look like what it's called: a field notebook.

- **Paper.** A faint dot grid sits behind every page, like a notebook.
- **Stamps.** Post dates and project diary dates are pressed on like a rubber stamp.
- **Tape.** The demos on the Ideas pages are taped to the page.
- **A sticky note.** The little box in the corner is now a yellow sticky note. On phones it waits until you've scrolled, so it never covers what you came to read.
- **Three doors.** The home page now asks where you want to start: new to AI, running a small business, or curious about what's possible.

The handwriting is a stand-in for now. Next, it gets replaced with a font made from my own handwriting, along with notes in the margins of posts and a proper photo on the About page.
