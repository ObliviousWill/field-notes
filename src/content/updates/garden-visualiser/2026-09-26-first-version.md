Most garden plans are flat drawings. They're accurate, but it's hard to imagine standing in them.

This keeps a flat plan, because it's the easiest way to draw, and puts a live 3D view right next to it. Draw a flower bed on the plan and it appears in the garden straight away.

The seasons and growth sliders matter most. A new garden looks thin on day one. Seeing what it will look like in year three, in June, is what tells you whether an idea is worth doing.

There's no AI inside it. It's a single page that runs in the browser, with nothing to install and no account needed.
