Most garden designers show clients a flat, technical drawing. It's accurate, but it's hard for most people to read.

This keeps the flat plan, because the designer needs it, and puts a live 3D view right next to it. Draw a flower bed on the plan and it appears in the garden straight away.

The seasons and growth sliders matter most. A new garden looks thin on day one. Showing a client what it will look like in year three, in June, is what helps them say yes. It also stops them being disappointed on day one.

There's no AI inside it. It's a single page that runs in the browser, with nothing to install and no account needed.
