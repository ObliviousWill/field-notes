The business launched a new booking app for customers earlier this year, and it's useful for everyone to see how it's getting on.

So there's now a second page that refreshes itself every Tuesday morning. It shows:

- how many bookings come through the app, week by week
- how many customers are new and how many already knew us
- which products people book through it
- where the app is being used most, so we can share what's working

The checks are strict. The same set of questions is asked word for word every week, and a short script checks the answers before anything is published. Totals must agree with each other, and running totals can't suddenly shrink. If a check doesn't pass, the page stays as it was and I get a message explaining which check needs a look.

One small design touch: the current week is drawn with a dashed line, so a week that's only half finished doesn't look like a dip.
