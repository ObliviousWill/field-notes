pubDate: 'Oct 03 2026'

The business launched a booking app for parents in late May. The question everyone kept asking was simple: is it working?

So there's now a second page that refreshes itself every Tuesday morning. It answers the questions people actually ask:

- How many bookings come through the app, and is that growing?
- Are these new customers or existing ones who switched?
- When a payment fails, is the card being declined, or is the parent giving up halfway through?
- Which local franchises have got their parents using it, and which haven't started?
- Which schools have no app bookings yet? That is the quickest list of places to help.

The payment question turned out to be the useful one. Most failed payments weren't declined cards. They were parents leaving the checkout. That is a design problem, not a payments problem, and it is far cheaper to fix.

The checks are tighter than the daily report's. The same twelve questions are asked word for word every week, and a short script checks the answers before anything is published. Totals must agree with each other, and running totals can't suddenly shrink. If a check fails, the page stays as it was and I get a message saying which check failed and why.

One small design touch: the current week is drawn with a dashed line. Otherwise a half-finished week looks like a collapse.
