PISTA is live at [/pista/](/field-notes/pista/). It has its own full-screen, dark look, with one small link back to Field Notes in the corner.

## What it does

- **Four ways to play.** Americano and Mexicano, where partners change every round and you score for yourself. Fixed pairs, where everyone plays everyone. Or a single match with proper sets.
- **It draws the rounds for you.** It avoids putting the same partners together twice and the same opponents against each other too often. When the numbers don't divide by four, it makes sure the same person doesn't keep sitting out. If you don't like a draw, you can ask for another.
- **A live table.** Points, wins, and an all-time table across every night you've played.
- **Late arrivals and early leavers.** You can change who's playing between rounds without starting again.

## Where your scores are kept

There's no account and nothing is stored online. Everything is saved in your phone's browser.

That's a trade-off. Your scores are there when you come back on the same phone, but they don't follow you to another one, and clearing your browser data wipes them. So there's a backup button that lets you copy or download everything and restore it later. If your browser won't save anything at all, as happens in private browsing, a warning tells you straight away, rather than you losing a night's scores.

## Why it's fast

It's one page with nothing extra to load. That keeps it working with one bar of signal at the side of a court. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen, like a normal app.
