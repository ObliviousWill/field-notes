I measured the app on phone-sized screens instead of guessing. The screen you use during a game was almost two screens tall, and longer still with twelve players. Whatever you needed was always a scroll away.

## What changed

- **The round and the table are now two tabs** instead of one long page. Each one fits on a screen.
- **The main button stays at the bottom of the screen**: "Next round", "Start match" and so on. It used to sit at the end of the page, out of sight just when you needed it.
- **Things you rarely do moved into a menu** (⋯): redraw the round, change who's playing, delete the night. "Delete" is no longer right next to the scores.
- **The round number stays visible** at the top while you scroll.
- **Players sitting out show as small faces** rather than a long list of names.
- **"Undo" pops up at the bottom**, where your thumb already is.
- **It loads faster.** The fonts were holding up the first screen.

## Result

The game screen now fits exactly one screen on every phone I tested, and on a computer too. The players list still scrolls a little on small phones, which is fine. Lists are meant to scroll.
