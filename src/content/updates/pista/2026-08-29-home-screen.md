When you add PISTA to an iPhone's home screen, it opens full screen like a real app. That changes the shape it has to fit. There's no browser bar, so you get more room, but the app now runs right up behind the clock at the top and the bar at the bottom. The title and the round number were hiding behind the clock.

## The fix

- **Every screen now leaves room** for the clock at the top and the bar at the bottom. It does the same on the sides when you turn the phone round.
- **Things are a bit tighter**, so the main screens fit without scrolling. On short phones it trims the spacing, never the buttons. Buttons stay big enough for a thumb.
- **A backup plan.** Some iPhones don't tell the app how much room the clock takes. If that happens, the app works it out from the phone's screen size instead.

I measured it on five iPhones, from the biggest to the small SE. Everything fits on every one, except one set-up screen on the SE, which is a hair too tall.
