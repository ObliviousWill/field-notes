Three new things, all built on one idea. What brings a padel group back isn't the app. It's the banter in the group chat afterwards. So the app should be good at feeding that, and stay out of the way during a game.

## A result picture you can send

"Copy the table" gave you plain text, and nobody pastes that. Now there's **Share the result**. It makes a picture of the night, with the winner, the full table and the date, and opens your phone's share menu. Straight into WhatsApp, which is where next week's game gets arranged.

I kept the picture small, about a fifteenth of the size it first came out at, so it sends quickly even on bad signal.

## Rivalries on court

The app remembers every result, so it knows who has beaten whom. Before a game, a line now appears on the court, such as *Ana & Ben lead 2–1*. It only appears once two people or pairs have met at least twice, so it means something when it does. It doesn't take any taps or extra space, and it disappears once you enter the score.

## A link for everyone else

The night used to live on one phone. Now **Share a view link** squeezes the whole night into a single web link. Anyone who opens it sees the table, the winner and every round, but can't change anything. If they want it on their own phone, they tap **Save to my device**. Players with the same name are matched up, so nobody ends up listed twice.

There's still no server and no account. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.
