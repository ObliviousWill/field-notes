Three additions, all pointed at the same idea: the thing that brings a padel group back is not the app, it is the argument in the group chat afterwards. So make the app good at feeding that, and stay out of the way during a round.

## A result card you can actually send

"Copy the table" produced plain text, which nobody pastes. There is now **Share the result**: the app draws the night on a canvas, dark and on brand, with the winner, the full table and the date, and hands it to the phone's share sheet as an image. Straight into WhatsApp, where the next Thursday gets arranged.

It renders as a JPEG rather than a PNG: the card is mostly gradient, so PNG came out at 1.19MB against 76KB for a JPEG nobody can tell apart. Where the share sheet is not available, the image downloads instead. Quick matches get their own card with the set scores.

## Rivalries above the court

Every result is already stored, so the app knows who has beaten whom. Before a round is scored, the court now carries a line: *Ana & Ben lead 2–1* when those two pairs have met before, and otherwise the strongest personal rivalry on court, *Ana leads Ben 4–2*, picked from the players facing each other. It needs two previous meetings before it says anything, so it means something when it appears.

It costs no taps and no vertical space: it sits in the row that already held the court number, and it disappears once the score goes in.

## A view link for everyone not scoring

The real limit was that the night lived on one phone. **Share a view link** packs the whole event, players and all, into a URL. A finished eight player night comes to 672 characters: the payload is gzipped through `CompressionStream` and base64url encoded, with a plain base64 fallback for browsers without it.

Whoever opens it gets a read only view, clearly marked as a snapshot, with the table, the winner and every round. Nothing touches their own data unless they tap **Save to my device**, which imports the night and merges players by name so nobody ends up with two Anas. Saving the same link twice opens what you already have rather than making a copy.

No server, no accounts, nothing leaving anyone's phone except the link they choose to send.
