title: 'Singles, and all three pairings'
pubDate: 'Aug 29 2026'
---

Two things wrong with Quick match, both reported from an actual court.

**Swap only ever offered two of the three pairings.** With four players there are three ways to split them: AB v CD, AC v BD, AD v BC. The old button rebuilt the line-up as `[a1, b1, a2, b2]`, which flips to the second pairing and then flips straight back, so the third was unreachable no matter how many times you tapped. It now holds the first player and rotates who partners them, so tapping walks all three and comes back round. Relabelled "Change pairs", because that is what it does.

**Singles were not possible.** Quick match insisted on exactly four players. It now takes two or four: two gives you one a side, four gives you the pairs. Everything downstream already worked with a team of one, so sets, games per set, golden point and the super tiebreak all carry over unchanged, and the match page says Singles or Doubles rather than just Match.

Picking three players leaves the start button out, with a line explaining why.
