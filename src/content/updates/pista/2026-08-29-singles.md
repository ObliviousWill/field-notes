pubDate: 'Aug 29 2026'
---

Two problems with Quick match, both found on an actual court.

**The "swap" button only ever gave two of the three pairings.** Four players can split into pairs three ways. The old button flipped between two of them and never reached the third. Now each tap moves through all three in turn. The button is called "Change pairs", because that's what it does.

**You couldn't play singles.** Quick match only worked with exactly four players. Now it takes two or four. Scoring works the same either way, and the match screen says "Singles" or "Doubles".

If you pick three players, the start button stays hidden and a line explains why.
