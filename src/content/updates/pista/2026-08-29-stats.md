project: 'pista'
title: 'Quick matches count now'
pubDate: 'Aug 29 2026'
---

Reported: player stats were not updating. They were updating, but only from events. `profileStats` and the all time table both walked `S.events` and never looked at `S.matches`, so you could win a quick match 2 sets to 1 and your profile would still say "Yet to play" with zeros across the board.

The reason it was built that way is a real problem rather than an oversight: the two formats score in different units. An Americano round is points, 21 to 13. A match is games inside sets, 6–4 6–7 6–3. Adding 6 games to 21 points gives a number that means nothing, so the fix is not to merge them into one total.

So matches are counted, in their own units:

- **The profile** now has two blocks. "Event rounds" keeps points, win rate, difference, rounds, won and lost. "Matches" adds played, won and a sets tally. Whichever you have not played simply does not appear, so nobody sees a column of misleading zeros. Recent form now mixes both, ordered by when they actually happened, and the best partner and toughest opponent records count match results too.
- **The players list** mentions matches in each person's line.
- **The home screen** has two boards where it had one, "All-time · events" and "All-time · matches", so a night of quick matches shows up rather than leaving the screen blank.
