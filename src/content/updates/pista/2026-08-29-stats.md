Someone told me their player stats weren't updating. They were, but only from tournament nights. You could win a quick match and your profile would still say "Yet to play".

There was a reason, and it's a real one. The two kinds of game are scored differently. A tournament round is scored in points, like 21–13. A match is scored in games and sets, like 6–4, 6–3. Add one to the other and you get a number that means nothing.

So now each kind is counted separately:

- **Your profile** has two sections: tournament rounds, with points and win rate, and matches, with played, won and sets. If you haven't played one kind, that section just doesn't appear, so you never see a row of misleading zeros.
- **Recent form** mixes both, in the order you played them.
- **The home screen** has two all-time tables, one for each kind of game.
