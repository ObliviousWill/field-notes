The boiler came first. Every part is built from simple shapes in code, so the page is small and loads quickly, even on a weak phone signal.

The same day I made a second demo: a **planner for a tiny downstairs toilet**. You drag the toilet, basin, mirror and towel rail around a 3D room, and it tells you, as you go, whether everything fits:

- Is there at least 60cm of space in front of the toilet?
- Does the door hit anything when it opens?
- Is the doorway wide enough for a standard door?

It shows "Everything fits", "Fits, with two things to check" or "Three problems to fix". That's the sort of check a plumber would otherwise do with a tape measure on a second visit.

[Try the bathroom planner](/field-notes/demos/bathroom/). Your layout is only saved in your own browser.
