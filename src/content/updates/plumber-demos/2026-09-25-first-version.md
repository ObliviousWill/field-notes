Every part of the boiler is built from simple shapes in code, with no 3D files to download. That keeps the page small, so it loads quickly even on a weak phone signal.

Each of the 17 parts answers the same four questions: what it does, what goes wrong, what you'll notice, and how it's fixed. Asking the same four questions every time makes it easy to scan, whichever part you tap.

The symptom buttons were the part I cared about most. Customers don't think "my photocell has failed". They think "the reset light keeps coming on". So you start from what you can see, and the boiler shows you where to look.
