The holiday planner came first. Our policy encourages everyone to spread their holiday through the year, so they get proper breaks and don't end up with days left over at the end.

Now each person has a simple bar. Days already taken are solid and days booked are striped, with a marker showing a sensible pace for the time of year. If anyone looks like they might not get to use all their holiday, it's easy to spot early and help them plan it in.

It also checks new requests against the team's agreed guidelines before they're approved, such as making sure not too many people are away at once. That keeps decisions consistent and fair for everyone.
