The holiday planner came first. Our policy asks people to spread their holiday through the year, with checkpoints every quarter. Until now, nobody could actually see how each person was doing against those checkpoints.

Now each person has a simple bar. Days already taken are solid and days booked are striped. A line marks where they should be by now. The page writes a short list of who to talk to and why, ready to copy into my one-to-one notes. It only lists people who need a conversation.

It also checks new holiday requests against the team's rules before I say yes: too many people off at once, people who cover for each other off together, and the busy weeks when nobody should be away.
