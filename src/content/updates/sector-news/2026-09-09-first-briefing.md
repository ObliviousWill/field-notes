pubDate: 'Sep 16 2026'

The first few briefings came as a file attached to a notification. Attachments are easy to miss in a busy inbox.

So the whole briefing now goes in the body of the email: the three stories, why each matters, and the links. The file is still there for anyone who wants it, but the email is written to be read on its own, on a phone, in under two minutes.

Lesson: deliver things where people already look.
